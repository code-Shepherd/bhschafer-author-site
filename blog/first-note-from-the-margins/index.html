First Note from the Margins

May 12, 2026

A short update on the author site, current projects, and the road ahead.

This is where the blog post begins. The tone here can be looser than the formal essays: part update, part reflection, part author’s desk note.

I have been thinking about the difference between building a platform and building a home. A platform asks what it can extract from attention. A home asks what kind of person might return.

On the Site

The author site is beginning as a simple static place: a landing page, a works section, a newsletter doorway, and now this blog. Nothing bloated. Nothing pretending to be a publishing empire before the bricks are laid.

The point is not to appear established. The point is to become inevitable.

Over time, this space will collect updates on fiction, essays, publishing plans, progress on ongoing books, and the occasional stray thought that refuses to behave itself.

Current Work

The main creative priority remains fiction: revising, drafting, and building toward a body of work that can stand on its own without needing constant explanation.

More soon.
