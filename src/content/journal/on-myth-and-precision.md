Mythic tone is not vague tone. Grand themes do not excuse blurry sentences.

The larger your story's claims, the more precise your line-level writing must become. Readers trust a world when it has weight, and weight comes from detail: a chipped cup, a breath held too long, a staircase that groans in one specific place.

Precision is not the opposite of mystery. It is what makes mystery believable.
