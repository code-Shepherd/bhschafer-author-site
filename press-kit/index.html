Medium Bio

B. H. Schafer is a Computer Science graduate, speculative and literary fiction writer, and the voice behind Schafer's Quill. His short stories have appeared in PREE Literary Magazine. When he's not wrestling with daemons in the machine, he's etching words onto the page. He lives in Jamaica, beneath a highway, much to his chagrin.
