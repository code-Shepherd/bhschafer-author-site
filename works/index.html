Gods, Monsters, and a Fuzzytail: Ashfall

Gods, Monsters, and a Fuzzytail is about a boy named Eli Wisp, born with barely enough magic to survive in a world where magic is life and power. While everyone else has magic to spare, Eli has none to waste. Still, he dares to dream of becoming the greatest Maestro the world has ever known. This is his story.
